# Surface brief: homepage (index.html)

Scope: homepage of edwardattuati.com; every other page inherits the same world. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience and job: recruiters and companies deciding in seconds whether to contact Edward.
Action: write to edwardattuati@icloud.com (primary), open LinkedIn/GitHub (secondary).
Proof: four real apps (Chrono, Memive, Scadoo, Blackjack Web), timeline 2015-2025, real GitHub numbers from the API.
Constraints: keep interactive atom, Konami + 20-click easter eggs, multilingual greeting, typewriter, 5-language i18n; remove chatbot and template scripts.
Memorable moment: the atom in a refracting glass bubble over the photograph (the earlier glass-droplet cursor was removed at Edward's request: it hid the text underneath).
Photographs (supplied by Edward, EXIF/GPS stripped): canal at sunset for home, sitemap, Chrono and Scadoo privacy; Kyoto bamboo for Chi sono and Scadoo; New York at night for CV and Memive.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Edward's own warm photograph seen through Liquid Glass. Refuses the category default (dark neon dev portfolio with glow and mono labels) and its opposite (white Swiss card grid).

OWN-WORLD: a full-bleed golden-hour photograph is the ground. Real liquid glass (live backdrop blur and saturation, specular rim, edge refraction where supported) for nav pill, hero panel, buttons, atom bubble and dialogs. Warm sand sheets slide over the photo to carry dense reading. Palette sampled from the canal photo: ink/forest #23251B, paper #F1EEE2 and #E7E3D3, sun #DFC38E, sun-ink #7A5A1C, secondary ink #4F5144. Product pages keep the world and change only --accent (Chrono amber #8A5A12, Scadoo sage #3F5A1C, Memive sun-ink). Display: Instrument Serif. Text: Hanken Grotesk.

STORY: the recruiter sees a real person and real shipped apps in the first seconds, believes he builds complete products end to end, and writes to him.

FIRST VIEWPORT: photo fills the screen. Glass nav pill centered at top (EA mark, Progetti, Chi sono, Percorso, Contatti, CV, language); under 820px a floating glass tab bar at the bottom. Left glass panel about 46% wide: greeting line cycling 38 languages in serif italic, name "Edward Attuati" at display scale, typewriter role line below the name (no eyebrow above it), one short description, dark-glass primary "Contattami" and clear-glass "Vedi i progetti". Right: the interactive atom inside a refracting glass bubble with its state chips. A "Scorri" glass chip sits above the first paper sheet's edge. Primary action lives in the panel.

FORM: "Vetro su luce calda", Liquid Glass over warm photography; position 1 of the ordered grounded list (IMPECCABLE'S PICK) on re-roll round 1; seed key 7d1df4ce. Signature interaction: the refracting glass atom bubble (click, drag, 20-click easter egg); the system cursor is kept. Motion grammar: spring easing cubic-bezier(.32,.72,0,1), glass that squashes on press, sections rising from a soft blur, sheets sliding over the photo.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
